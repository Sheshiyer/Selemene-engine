# Noesis render source handoff

Updated 4 November 2026 while consolidating the source repositories.

The selected visual direction is **Codex Dark**, chosen on 29 October 2026: dark matte patinated bronze, fine gold lines, emerald glass accents and a hexagonal plinth on a dark ground. The brighter Codex set is retained for reference.

## Source inventory

- [Codex Dark](../../design/renders/codex-dark/README.md): 27 PNG originals, their complete prompt manifest and an optional lens-position helper.
- [Codex reference](../../design/renders/codex/README.md): 27 first-pass PNG originals and their complete prompt manifest.

Each set has 17 engines, six workflows, three voice plates and one trinity-hex mark. Engines and workflows are 1024 × 1536 pixels; voice plates are 1536 × 1024; the mark is 1024 × 1024. All 54 PNGs retain their original bytes and metadata. Together they occupy about 112.4 MiB; the largest is under 4 MiB. They are stored in ordinary Git without a new LFS dependency.

## Scope and provenance

The original design-session handoff, Render Bible, figure experiments and related historical source are retained in the private recovery archive. They are historical references, not dependencies installed by this asset commit. This handoff contains no private artifact-share links and makes no claim that an external design system has been republished or verified.

The images are generated design assets. Prompt manifests preserve their production brief; historical CLI notes do not establish a currently available model, account or generation route. The optional lens detector provides estimates that need visual review. No new image generation or component acceptance is established by repository consolidation.

Selemene Engine owns these reusable engine/workflow brand assets. The private daily application, personal chart material, saved readings and its deployment remain in the independent `Sheshiyer/noesis-daily` repository.
