# Selemene Engine — a companion for symbolic reflection

- Proposed route: https://www.tryambakam.space/selemene/
- Website destination: `public/selemene/index.html`
- Prepared: 2026-10-07 for the free 0.2.0 connector.

Selemene helps you choose a reflection focus, discover supported engines, prepare accurate inputs and request a reading in your ChatGPT conversation. It supplies engine calculations, local symbolic draws and static framework meanings. Your current ChatGPT model supplies interpretation and the final prose using your applicable ChatGPT limits.

## Choose a focus; prepare what is needed

Start with the question you want to explore and your preferred language and level of detail. Discover the current engine catalog and inspect the requirements for your chosen reading. Preparation identifies missing fields before calculation. An unknown birth time, location or timezone stays unknown until you supply it; it is not guessed.

## Free sponsored access

The free Selemene connector requires no additional Selemene login, personal account or personal API key. The hosted service uses a sponsor credential kept on the server. Access depends on the connecting ChatGPT surface, available engines and service quotas.

**Before requesting a calculation:** submitted birth inputs and calculated results may be stored under a shared Selemene service/admin account. Calculations may update that account's profile, history, usage, XP or phase. This does not create a personal linked account or provide separate private history. Saving is asynchronous; a successful calculation does not confirm that a record was saved.

## Request a reading

Discussing or preparing a reading does not run it. Calculation follows your explicit request for the selected reading after the shared-service storage disclosure is understood. Results are shown with their actual source, missing fields and partial outcomes. Do not repeatedly retry an ambiguous calculation: another call can create another account effect or stored reading.

## Values, meanings and interpretation

Calculated values and local Tarot/I Ching draws remain distinct from ChatGPT's symbolic interpretation. Aletheios and Pichet are writing perspectives within your current conversation. The connector does not call another hosted narration model, the witness/report generator, a media analyzer or a paid generation endpoint.

Readings support reflection and self-inquiry. They do not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, guarantee future events or replace professional advice. Detailed prose and requested host-native exports depend on the tools available in your ChatGPT conversation; no independent certification is claimed.
