## Purpose and supported surface

Selemene supports symbolic reflection in ChatGPT through discovery, input preparation, explicitly requested engine/workflow calculations and a separate natal Vedic D1/D9 chart. Returned values and local symbolic draws are distinguished from interpretation. Readings do not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, guarantee outcomes or establish professional authority.

The connector does not expose account administration, private account history, media analysis, billing operations or paid generation. It does not run the hosted witness/report pipeline or claim independent certification. Detailed readings are composed by the user's current ChatGPT model; exports depend on host-native tools actually available and verified.

## Inputs and explicit execution

Engine requirements determine the fields needed for a calculation. Unknown birth time, coordinates or timezone are identified before execution rather than invented. Discussion and preparation alone do not run a reading. An ordinary calculation or workflow follows the user's explicit request after the shared-service storage disclosure is understood.

**Submitted inputs and calculated results can persist under the shared Selemene service/admin account and may update its profile, history, usage, XP or phase. This creates no personal linked account or separate private user history.** Persistence is asynchronous; a successful response is not proof that storage succeeded. The connector provides no personal history or deletion operation. Retention/deletion arrangements remain **PENDING**, as set out in the privacy draft.

The separate chart handler does not save a personal reading/history through that route, but this does not establish end-to-end no-storage processing. Its returned D1/D9 fields do not support absent combustion, dignities, yogas, Atmakaraka or Darakaraka findings.

## Availability, quotas and ambiguous results

The current service uses default fixed-window limits of 120 requests and 30 calculation attempts per connecting IP per minute; 600 requests and 60 calculation attempts for the service per minute; and 1,000 calculation attempts for the service per UTC day. These are configurable service defaults. Shared networks or ChatGPT gateways may share an IP. These limits do not establish personal account balances or guaranteed per-person fairness.

Calculation attempts are charged after known-tool and outer-input validation. Later preflight/backend failure is not refunded. Availability can differ by engine and workflow. Missing outputs and partial results must remain explicit; successful source or catalog discovery does not guarantee successful calculation.

The service has bounded request/result sizes and timeouts. Follow retry guidance actually returned. A calculation timeout can leave an uncertain persistence outcome; silently repeating it may create duplicate stored readings or account effects. The free connector has no personal history tool to confirm an ambiguous record.
