# Compass — Fig 4

A four-pan balance on a hexagonal bronze plinth (flat-to-flat, gold-edged, on six feet with corner screws, a compass rose engraved round the column foot, a label plate and a seat for the one weight). A turned column with a knurled collar and a table rises to a cup that carries the gold pivot ball; a cross beam with a hub cup rests on the ball, and from each arm end a stem and a hook ball hang a pan on three chains to eyelets on its rim. The pans are the four directions, placed per the Render Bible's settled call (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-noesis-render-bible-design.md`, "Compass directions"): STABILIZE on the left pan, HEAL near, CREATE right, MUTATE far; the names are the four of `docs/design/directions/README.md`. What moves: hovering over a pan (or ArrowLeft/Down/Right/Up; Home or Escape takes it out) carries the 1 g weight from its seat into that pan, and the beam tilts toward it as a real two-axis lever (`beam.mjs`): arm 50, hooks 6.3 below the pivot, pans 10 g, beam 12 g with its mass 4.5 above the ball, g in units of 2 mm, so one weight settles the beam at 50/233 rad = 14.0° (0.46 Hz, damping ratio 0.75, one small overshoot); the chains stay plumb, the pans drop and rise with their hooks, and a pointer with a bob hangs from the hub rim and reads the tilt on a sector scale (5°, 10°, 15° arcs with azimuth ticks) engraved on the column's table. The readout takes its word, direction and angle from the same instant of the model: `placing · HEAL`, `swinging · HEAL · 9°`, `settles · HEAL · 14°`, `lifting · HEAL · 14°`, `levelling · 3°`, `level`.
