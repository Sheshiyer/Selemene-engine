# Dyad chamber (Fig 2)

A bronze rail on a footed base plate with a ruler strip and a label plate carries two facing mirrors on carriages at its ends, each held in a yoke of two posts and trunnion blocks with a knurled tilt screw and a knurled lock knob: at the far end Pichet, a hexagonal mirror with a gold rim, reflection chords across its bronze face; at the near end Aletheios, a vesica frame whose emerald glass pane (`#10B5A7`, the one second colour this figure is allowed) is seen through the back aperture. Between them a small lamp, the only `lit` part, hangs from a jointed gold arm (post, knuckle, raked boom, knuckle, stem) on its own carriage with a knurled lead knob, and throws a gold pool on the rail. Dragging the lamp along the rail (pointer, with pointer capture on press; arrow keys step 5, Shift 20, Home and End go to either mirror, Space or 0 to the midpoint) moves it, and the mirror it is nearer lights while the other dims: the speaking rim takes the lit accent in its own colour (gold-warm for Pichet, emerald for Aletheios), its face brightens, and the other mirror's rim and face fall to the design system's `dimmed-witness` opacity of 0.35 through a void-coloured veil; at the midpoint both hold half and the readout says `held · both`. The idle tour, after the usual 1.4 s and 3.6 s after the last touch, swings the lamp slowly end to end on an 11 s sine; reduced motion holds it still, and the loop stops offscreen. The readout (`pichet speaks · 0.82`, `aletheios speaks · 0.61`, `held · both`) is the real distance ratio from the figure's own geometry: the speaker's share is the lamp's distance from the far mirror's face over the whole gap of 232 units between the faces at x = ±116, so the 75-unit travel gives 0.82 at either end and 0.50 at the centre, with a 0.02 band around 0.50 reported as held. The witness names and their symbols come from the design system's DyadSpeaker guideline (Pichet: sacred-gold, hexagon, structure; Aletheios: coherence-emerald, vesica, naming; the non-speaker dims to `dimmed-witness`) and from the Render Bible's self-inquiry station and its settled call that Pichet lights gold and Aletheios lights emerald; both names are the repo's Witness Dyad (`crates/engine-biofield/src/witness.rs`, `crates/noesis-witness/src/routing.rs` with `PichetPrimary`).
