# Engine mandala

A bronze bench plate, a flat-to-flat hexagon on six feet with six corner screws, a bevelled gold edge, an engraved groove, a gold rule strip and a label plate, milled with a 19-cell honeycomb (1 + 6 + 12, flat to flat): each pocket holds a shallow hexagonal bronze tray with a gold rim and a bronze tile seated in its well, clipped to its pocket so the part below the bench top hides behind the web as it would. The centre tray is Witness (the dyad service behind `/api/v1/witness/interpret`, not an engine): taller, carrying three interlocking gold rings with a small emerald glass lens where they overlap. The seventeen engine trays spiral outward from the inner ring in the order of `docs/ENGINES.md` (panchanga … raaga, parsed from the file at build time, so the readout's ids and `n/17` come from the repo), and the spiral is engraved into the webs between consecutive cells; the nineteenth pocket, at the end of the spiral, is empty and reads "the question". What moves: one tray at a time lifts 3.2 units in its pocket on an eased follow (τ 0.22 s), its rim and top light with the single `gold-warm` accent and a gold glow spills into the pocket; that lift is "the engine under the pointer". Hover or tap picks the nearest cell, arrow keys walk the ring (Home is Witness, End the question), the idle tour walks all nineteen every 1.5 s after 3.6 s of quiet, the loop stops offscreen and jumps with no tour under reduced motion. Size tier: Figure, 51 solids (plate, 12 foot parts, 18 trays, 17 tiles, lens boss, strip, label) plus 19 milled pockets and 17 wells drawn as recesses, 779 paths. Checks: `node ../.scripts/lines.mjs` on the dark page, the light page and the lifted pose (two ArrowRight presses, `shots/lines-lifted.png`) each print `line ends: 0 of 416 ends on 173 lines float`; every capture in `shots/` (desktop, 390 px phone, hover in both themes, keys, reduced motion, tour at +1.5 s and +3 s, an 8-frame motion sheet across a tour step, and 4× close-ups of the centre, a lifted cell, the empty pocket, the rule strip, the label plate and a foot, in both themes) reports `console: clean` and no sideways scroll. The kit audit (`kit/audit.mjs`) was not wired: the figure has no pipes, cables or turned bodies beyond the lens boss, and painter's order is settled by depth sort with the pocket clips. Build: `node build.mjs` (dark, also writes `preview.html`), `node build.mjs --light`.
