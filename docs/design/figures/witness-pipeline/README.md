# Witness pipeline (Fig 3, Hero)

A bronze bench with a flat belt over two drums and a hand crank, four C-frame stations reaching over the belt from the far side, a hopper of blank plates over the head of the belt and a tray of folded pages at the tail. A plate drops from the hopper and rides the belt; the crank and the left drum's spokes turn with it. Station 1 is a die press (screw, knurled handwheel with turning spokes, ram, die) that stamps the engine's number into the plate; station 2 is a switch gate whose gold lever, the one lit part, tilts on its index quadrant to the route and moves a punch carriage across the belt to punch that route into the plate's edge; station 3 is a reading lens (knurled barrel, eyepiece) with two pen arms that drop together and write two lines at once; station 4 is a folio press whose platen descends on two guide rods and whose blade folds the plate into a page with a crease, one line and a dot. Hover scrubs the plate along the belt, ← → step it between the six stops, ↑ ↓ change the engine, Home/End go to the ends; the idle tour (after 1.4 s, resumes 3.6 s after the last touch) runs one plate through per engine, seventeen engines in `docs/ENGINES.md` order, then fades it and drops the next blank. The readout names the stage the plate is in and what it carries so far (`routing · vimshottari · #04 A`): the stage names and the plate's impressions come from `crates/noesis-witness/src`: `generate_witness_prompt` in `lib.rs` takes the engine output in; `routing_for_engine` in `routing.rs` returns `AletheiosPrimary` (A, lever left), `PichetPrimary` (P, lever right) or `DyadSynthesis` (A+P, lever straight; raaga is unmapped and falls into the dyad bin as `partition_by_routing` does); `interpret_with_llm` in `interpret.rs` runs the Aletheios and Pichet pillars in parallel with `tokio::join!`, which is why two pens write at once; and its third call, `complete_for_role("synthesis")` through `parse_synthesis_json`, returns the `WitnessDyadLlm` with `synthesis` and `witness_question`, the folded page. The engraved number is the engine's position in `docs/ENGINES.md` (1–17), not a computed engine value, since the crate carries engine output as opaque `serde_json::Value`. Size tier Hero: 127 drawn parts, 315 KB per page, scale 1.25. Checks: `lines.mjs` prints `line ends: 0 of 300` on the dark page and `0 of 300` on the light page at rest, and `0 of 302` on both themes in the outfeed pose with the page folded; console clean on every capture; no sideways scroll at 390 px; 4× close-ups of the hopper, each station with the plate under its head, and the crank and outfeed were shot in both themes (`shots/dark-*.png`, `shots/light-*.png`) and read; the kit's geometry audit (`kit/audit.mjs`) is not wired because the figure has no pipes, cables or exploded assemblies, so painter's order is set by layer (far posts and gussets, belt, plate, then near rail, drums, heads, crossbars) and was checked at 4× in every pose rather than measured.

**Stage source.** The Render Bible's call was four stations named "engine output → routing → interpret → Folio". The crate shows a different last stage: there is no Folio anywhere in `crates/noesis-witness` (Folio is a surface of the TypeScript `packages/witness-pipeline`, per the repo README); the crate's own final step is the `synthesis` role in `llm.rs` `model_for_tier` and `SYNTHESIS_SYSTEM` in `interpret.rs`, whose output is the `WitnessDyadLlm` struct. The figure's stations are therefore **engine output → routing → interpret → synthesis**, named exactly so in the readout, and the fourth press folds the plate into the synthesis page rather than a Folio.
