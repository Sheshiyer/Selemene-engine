# Integrated Consciousness Reading

A comprehensive multi-engine reading that synthesizes insights from Vedic astrology,
Human Design, Gene Keys, and biorhythm into a unified consciousness snapshot.

## Engine Selection

This mode uses all seven primary engines:

1. **Vimshottari Dasha** (Aletheios) - Temporal cycles and planetary periods
2. **Human Design** (Aletheios) - Bodygraph gates and channels
3. **Gene Keys** (Dyad) - Shadow/Gift/Siddhi activation sequences
4. **Panchanga** (Dyad) - Today's cosmic weather (tithi, nakshatra, yoga)
5. **Biorhythm** (Pichet) - Physical, emotional, intellectual cycles
6. **Transits** (Dyad) - Current planetary influences
7. **Numerology** (Aletheios) - Name and birth number currents

## Interpretation Flow

1. Aletheios synthesizes the structural patterns (Dasha, HD gates, numbers)
2. Pichet synthesizes the somatic/vitality layer (biorhythm)
3. Both pillars weave the cross-domain engines (panchanga, gene keys, transits)
4. Final synthesis holds the tension between stillness and movement

## Consciousness Level Gating

- Level 0-1: Basic pattern recognition, no shadow work
- Level 2: Include Gene Keys shadow descriptions
- Level 3+: Full activation sequence with siddhi potentials
