## opening-pass
Use the supplied relationship header and mapping goal. Name the three declared business partners and explain the report's evidence/interpretation key. Keep individual agency explicit; do not promise business outcomes or imply the chart decides their roles.

## source-map-pass
Use a source-by-subject table. Distinguish Selemene engine facts, attributed HumDes cross references, and user-provided image evidence. State dates or uncertainty only where explicitly supplied. No source substitution or general chart claims.

## signatures-pass
Present a detailed but readable chart signature for each person. Include a source-labeled fact table for type, profile, authority, definition, centres, complete channels, every personality/design planet-point gate.line activation present in that person's engine-facts block, active gates, cross gate-lines, and cross name when supplied. Do not omit listed rows or claim a row is absent when it is present in the facts. Add short, clearly marked interpretive notes only where the source pack supplies authorized meaning text; preserve each person's distinct voice and limits.

## mechanics-pass
Compare exact type, profile, authority, and definition labels in a table. Follow with a bounded interpretation of similarities and differences, naming the source inputs and marking all interpretation as tentative. Do not prescribe decision styles, governance, or role allocation.

## channels-pass
List each person's supplied defined/undefined centres and complete channels. Identify only identical channel strings present in multiple engine records. Never infer bridging, connections, or compatibility from gates.

## pairwise-pass
Create exactly three pairwise rows. Compute each shared active-gate set by set intersection of the exact source lists. Report identical complete-channel strings separately. Add a compact evidence-led reading for each pair that names only authorized symbolic meanings present in the source pack, marks interpretation as tentative, and does not treat shared gates as channels, compatibility scores, or outcomes.

## penta-pass
Transcribe the participant legend, displayed ages, gates, function headings, and axes into source-attributed lists from the attached image only. State that it does not establish current ages, roles, responsibilities, or performance. If absent, say so and do not reconstruct it.

## timing-pass
Use a table of the dated Panchanga and Vimshottari outputs supplied for each partner. Identify the calculation snapshots. Do not add meanings, themes, forecasts, causal claims, or business timing.

## overlays-pass
Use source-attributed tables for supplied Gene Keys and numerology values. Do not explain the labels, infer personal qualities, validate one system with another, or rank partner contribution.

## collaboration-pass
Provide an evidence matrix of exact repeated or differing source values with source locations, then a clearly separate interpretive commentary on what these symbolic contrasts may invite the partners to discuss. Do not claim this describes their actual behavior. Do not assign departments, jobs, leadership, decision authority, or business capability from symbolic labels.

## questions-pass
Begin with a compact evidence frame that names each partner and cites their exact engine-confirmed type/profile or complete-channel values. Then offer at most four short, open, balanced questions grounded in repeated or contrasting source values. Keep source labels distinct from claims about actual work: questions must not assume a role, capability, disagreement, shared experience, or meaning from a gate, channel, screenshot label, or timing period. Do not give business, legal, financial, investment, or governance advice. State the chart does not answer these questions.

## synthesis-pass
Synthesize only source-verified individual and pairwise facts already stated in this report. Do not recalculate any gate intersection; refer back to the pairwise evidence section. Separate stable design facts, explicitly dated engine snapshots, and tentative interpretive threads. Add no prediction, diagnosis, guarantee, compatibility verdict, role assignment, or new source value. Preserve the screenshot's limits. End with one optional question about how partners want to compare these records with their own work experience, followed by a complete sentence.
